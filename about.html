Bring your hometown

Earth Online is built to be extended. Fork the repo, draw a boundary, generate streets from real map data, and land a place that feels like yours. Small towns, big cities, quiet blocks — all welcome.

  • Start from the Real Map Lab and real OSM geometry
  • Keep the low-poly, real-time look of the SF chapter
  • Open a pull request so others can visit what you built
